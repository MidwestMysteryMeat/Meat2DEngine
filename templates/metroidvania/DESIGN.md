# Metroidvania starter

This template starts with:

- a deterministic side-view world with platforms and traversal space;
- a simple player controller with horizontal movement and jumping;
- nearest-neighbor SDL3 rendering;
- an engine-ready layout for rooms, checkpoints, abilities, enemies, and
  collectible progression.

Controls: `A`/`D` move, `W` jumps, and `Esc` quits.

Use the starter as the room-and-player slice, then add scene streaming,
abilities, combat, save points, and your own entity components.
