# Castlevania-style starter

This starter is for authored side-view action exploration: rooms, gates,
platform traversal, melee/projectile combat, enemy encounters, and a boss
progression. It deliberately differs from the generic side-scroller by
centering room-to-room metroidvania structure, gated abilities, and combat
state. Add tilemaps, animation sets, hitboxes, camera zones, checkpoints, and
save data on top of the public scene and physics APIs.
