# Falling-sand starter

This template is the focused cellular-simulation starter:

- a deterministic elements laboratory;
- sand, water, stone, fire, smoke, and other material interactions;
- fixed-step simulation and dirty-region rendering;
- mouse painting for quickly testing new materials and reactions.

Controls: left mouse paints, right mouse erases, `1` selects sand, `2` water,
`3` stone, `4` fire, and `Esc` quits.

Use this template when cellular materials are the primary game mechanic. For
ordinary side-scrollers, top-down games, or metroidvanias, choose their
specialized starter instead.
